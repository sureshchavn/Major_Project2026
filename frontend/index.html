CLINICAL STAGING CRITERIA

Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy Scale

The five standardized diagnostic stages used by ophthalmologists to grade disease progression.

STAGE 0 Healthy

No DR

No visible retinal abnormalities, hemorrhages, or microaneurysms. Normal baseline fundus photography.

STAGE 1 Mild

Mild DR

Presence of microaneurysms only. Earliest clinically observable stage of vascular deterioration.

STAGE 2 Moderate

Moderate DR

Microaneurysms, dot/blot hemorrhages, hard exudates, or cotton wool spots exceeding mild criteria.

STAGE 3 Severe

Severe DR

> 20 intraretinal hemorrhages in each of 4 quadrants, definite venous beading, or prominent IRMA.

STAGE 4 Critical

Proliferative DR

Neovascularization of the retina or disc, vitreous hemorrhage, or fibrovascular proliferation. High vision risk.
